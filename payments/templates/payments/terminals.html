Real-Time Ingestion Architecture

This dashboard operates purely in Consuming Mode. Whenever a payment is completed on any Moniepoint POS hardware across your restaurant (card tap, card insert, or bank transfer), Moniepoint fires an instant webhook notification to your callback endpoint:

POST /api/webhooks/moniepoint/
Headers: X-Moniepoint-Signature: {HMAC_SHA256_HASH}
Payload: {
  "eventType": "V1_POS_TRANSACTION",
  "data": {
    "merchantReference": "REST-20260813-0941",
    "terminalSerial": "MP-BAR-01",
    "amount": 1250000,  // Kobo (₦12,500.00)
    "status": "SUCCESS",
    "paymentMethod": "CARD_PURCHASE"
  }
}
